Our hypothetical family story begins with a line of smiths, years of service to a royal palace and a Royal Warrant of Appointment. Craftsmanship, continuity and personal responsibility still guide how we care for the business and its wealth.

01 / The foundations

The courage to begin.

Generations of smiths built a reputation through skilled work and lasting commitments. That heritage grounds our values: craftsmanship, modest living and care for what the next generation will inherit.

02 / The responsibility

The care to carry it forward.

The business has grown through industrial change, and the family has accumulated wealth while continuing to live modestly. Today, digital tools help us coordinate the enterprise and our financial affairs with the same personal care.

03 / The possibility

The space to shape what comes next.

Learning, mentoring and the opportunity to contribute prepare the next generation for stewardship. The aim is to pass on judgement and purpose alongside ownership and wealth.

The work behind

Smith Family Office brings business affairs, estate planning and family wealth into one personal platform. Smaller than a traditional staffed single-family office, it keeps our oversight independent and our heritage intact, without joining a multi-family office.

From proof of concept to your own office

Fork it.
Run it on your own hardware.

Copy this prompt, paste it into your personal Claude, and it will fork the repository, wire in real data APIs and set the platform up for your own micro single-family office.

Fork https://github.com/Kartavya-Jharwal/FamilyBizness into my own GitHub account and set it up as a private micro single-family office on my own hardware. 1. Clone the fork and run it locally with Bun (bun install, bun run build, bun run preview). 2. Replace the prepared Smith demo records with my family's details, keeping every record browser-local or on my machine. 3. Wire in the real APIs I choose (accounting, banking or broker, market data) behind the existing Settings → Integrations previews. Keep keys out of the repository. 4. Keep the open-source licences and credits intact, and tell me plainly what still needs a qualified legal, tax or investment adviser. Start from this proof of concept; do not rebuild it from scratch.

Open by design

Built on open source.
