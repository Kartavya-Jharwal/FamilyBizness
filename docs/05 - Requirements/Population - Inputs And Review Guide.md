# Population - Inputs And Review Guide

Product identity: [Identity](../00%20-%20Canonical/Identity.md). Current implementation and verification: [Integration](../03%20-%20Architecture/Integration%20-%20Family%20Office%20Workspaces.md).

## Prepared office

Choose a visitor profile such as **Patriarch** or **Family technician** to enter the already-populated office. The [Smith Family Constitution](../00%20-%20Canonical/Smith%20Family%20-%20Constitution.md) owns the adopted family policy and the retained GBP numerical scenario. Visitor profiles share these records. Earlier Golden Bird and independent workspaces remain recoverable separately.

## Source material

All four supplied constitutions have been reviewed for selected application provisions. The prepared pack includes source identity, section, selected text, interpretation and assumption basis. Operating-source review labels identify the fictional application specification, not a historical family vote or legal approval. The user need not write or upload a new constitution to use the prepared office.

## Optional maintenance and repopulation

The following sequence is for deliberate administrative changes, not required onboarding.

1. **Family case:** enter the family, anchor business, case basis, reporting date/currency and selected source identities in `/office/case`.
2. **Governance:** replace participants, entity holding chains, ownership/voting, RACI, eligible approvers and continuity milestones. Stage edits, validate and save. Record sources and due dates; completion requires evidence.
3. **Sources and policy:** add selected excerpts, mark reviewed only after named review, bind typed rules in `/office/policy`, then activate. Source edits invalidate the active revision. Allocation limits, screens, annual distributions, designated reserves and approval thresholds have distinct units and scopes. Do not infer unsupported rules from prose.
4. **Financials:** populate source entity balance/income/cash records and opening equity/cash, FX rates and matched elimination IDs. Reconcile the statements. Define non-overlapping family wealth owners, the business carrying account to replace and the DCF/multiple assumptions. Keep foundation/restricted assets separate. Record dated AUM observations and monthly entity budgets; receipts must be explicit.
5. **Estate:** choose the supported dated rule pack through the event date; enter asset ownership, beneficiaries, debts, exemptions, relief eligibility and cash gifts. Confirm scoped legal assumptions before interpreting a total. Enter funding events and accessible cash. A trust lifecycle is a separate scenario, not automatically added to the personal estate calculation.
6. **Portfolio:** enter two to six securities/funds, classifications, current/minimum/maximum weights, full correlations, expected returns and risk/cost/tax assumptions. Apply source-linked screens and constraints, solve, inspect feasibility and compare outcomes. Enter dated projection costs, contributions and internal generation transfers separately. Calibrate assumptions rather than interpreting example paths as forecasts.
7. **Performance:** populate consecutive monthly valuations, opening-boundary flows, security-level fees/taxes, retained income, FX and benchmark weights/returns. Adjacent periods must reconcile. Enter period inflation and target return. Gross/net TWR and attribution are computed from these records, separately from AUM snapshots.
8. **Operations and capital:** populate obligation owners/dates/recurrence/evidence and provider quotes. Select the forecast budget year for grants/ventures; complete diligence, create the linked governance decision, record required approvals and approve the commitment. Payment/refund entries require external evidence; they do not send money.
9. **Reporting:** save current inputs before exporting. The full board pack reads saved owners and identifies missing/invalid inputs. Export its companion input snapshot and a case pack for handover.

## Checks before presentation

- Case identity, people/entity IDs, currencies, dates and source definitions agree across modules.
- Balance, cash, equity and elimination residuals are understood and resolved.
- Business valuation is included once; restricted money is not treated as spendable family cash.
- Policy findings show actual source IDs and current revisions; unreviewed assumptions remain visible.
- Sample labels are retained wherever figures are hypothetical. Reviewers/approvers are local assertions, not authenticated identities.
- Retain exports outside browser storage. Restores validate all included modules before replacement.

## Team submission inputs

Supply the chosen Assignment 1 extracts and case narrative, review hypothetical numerical assumptions, explain the results in the final family context, attach genuine AI-session evidence and team contributions, and submit via the VLE. The platform cannot manufacture source selection, historical prompt evidence or professor authorisation.
