# Pillars

Mandated product pillars. Product identity → [`../00 - Canonical/Identity.md`](../00%20-%20Canonical/Identity.md).

| # | Pillar | Intent |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | Governance & succession | Family board, succession pathways, continuity. |
| 2 | Constitution linkage | Extract/embed family constitution into platform logic with **traceability** (see Trace.md): ownership/shareholding, governance bodies, decision rights, employment policy, dividend/distribution, succession, conflict resolution. |
| 3 | Estate & wealth structuring | Structures and vehicles; **English law** baseline. |
| 4 | Portfolio selection & optimisation | Allocation and optimisation of investable capital. |
| 5 | Financial modelling & analytics | Financials, AUM, returns, use of capital, broader modelling. |
| 6 | SFO / MFO operating model | How the office runs (single- or multi-family). |

All six pillars now have functional workspaces in the main application. Current model ownership, supported scope and verification are recorded in [Integrated Workspaces](../03%20-%20Architecture/Integration%20-%20Family%20Office%20Workspaces.md). The [Whole Brief Acceptance](Acceptance%20-%20Whole%20Brief.md) defines what must be demonstrated; selected Assignment 1 sources remain a team population dependency.
