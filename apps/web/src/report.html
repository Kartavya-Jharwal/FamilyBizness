Open the family workspace

Governance and constitutional control

Participants, entity ownership chains, voting interests, RACI decision rights and continuity actions can be edited and saved. Decisions retain distinct approvers and evidence. Selected constitutional excerpts retain assignment identity, interpretation and review. Typed rules require explicit activation; changing a source invalidates its active revision.

Governance and succession · Source register · Executable policy

Estate and wealth structures

The estate workbench calculates supported English-law inheritance-tax scenarios using dated rule packs, gifts, exemptions, residence taper and business-relief eligibility inputs. A separate simple relevant-property trust model calculates entry, anniversary and exit charges. Transfer timing and monthly liquidity can be compared; unsupported cases withhold totals. Entity ownership and legal-access assumptions remain explicit.

Estate workbench and official rule sources

Financial records and family wealth

Entity statements reconcile balances, income, cash movements and equity. Matched intercompany records are eliminated, currencies translated using entered rates, and exceptions reported. A separate family wealth perimeter replaces the business carrying investment with DCF equity rather than adding it twice. Dated wealth snapshots track AUM, flows and drawdown. Monthly entity forecasts allocate distributions, reinvestment, philanthropy, ventures and succession reserves.

Financial statements, valuation and capital uses

Investment selection and analytics

The investment workspace solves continuous constrained allocations for two to six securities or funds, validates the full correlation matrix and reports infeasibility and numerical diagnostics. Sector screens, cash floors and exposure limits consume activated policy. Observed monthly records produce gross/net time-weighted returns, real returns, benchmark comparison, Sharpe, drawdown, historical VaR and linked attribution. Seeded correlated projections include fees, gains-tax assumptions, spending, illiquidity and dated generation transfers.

Portfolio construction and scenarios

Running the office

Obligations have owners, dates, independent recurring occurrences and completion evidence. Provider quotes produce SFO/MFO cost comparisons. Grant and venture requests move through diligence, linked governance approval, budget commitment and externally evidenced payment/refund records. The board pack reads saved module inputs and flags missing or invalid data.

Data and evidence

Example inputs are fictional until populated and reviewed. A source register entry is not a claim that a team assignment has been selected. The case workspace validates saved records and supports portable case packs. Reports preserve assumptions and input snapshots. The platform runs locally in the browser; it does not execute payments, trades, legal transfers or authenticated approvals. AI implementation notes and acceptance documentation accompany the source project.
