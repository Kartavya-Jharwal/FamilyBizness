FAMILY / ENTERPRISE / CONTINUITY

A family's story continues through its decisions.

FamilyBizness brings the work of stewardship into one office: who owns what, who may decide, how wealth moves between generations, and what the family must do next.

One platform, each family's own case

Create a family workspace with its own constitution, ownership structure, financial assumptions and responsibilities. Use a blank case or explore a supplied teaching example. Golden Bird is one optional assignment case in that library.

From values to action

Connect constitutional clauses to distribution safeguards, voting thresholds, investment constraints and the office calendar. Review the same saved records in estate scenarios, financial reports and the board pack.

Meet the next obligation

Schedule the Council and Board, assign work, record review evidence and compare succession funding. The portfolio workspace holds profile selection and setup together.

Choose or create a workspace

The MVP stores its fictional cases and edits in this browser. Export a case pack to retain or move your work.
