# Platform Design And Evaluation

## Purpose and case basis

The platform implements the [six mandated pillars](../05%20-%20Requirements/Pillars.md) in the existing [FamilyBizness application](../00%20-%20Canonical/Identity.md). Its purpose is to make governance and wealth decisions inspectable: who can decide, which constitutional rule applies, what financial consequences follow, and what must be funded or completed.

The current fictional application uses the [Smith Family Constitution](../00%20-%20Canonical/Smith%20Family%20-%20Constitution.md). Supplied constitutions were synthesised into that one family policy, which keeps the retained GBP numerical scenario. Visitor profiles share one prepared office; original source businesses and balance sheets stay separate. The team remains responsible for its final evaluation and institutional submission evidence.

## Governance and succession

The participant register separates economic and voting rights. An entity ownership graph calculates look-through economic interests and a defined majority-vote control test; it does not claim to infer every contractual or trust power. RACI rules constrain decision transitions and identify eligible approvers. Monetary thresholds can require multiple distinct recorded approvals. Continuity actions have owners, successors, dates, dependencies and evidence gates. Transfer scenarios preserve before/after interests without executing a legal transfer.

This design makes a succession plan operational: an incomplete prerequisite remains visible, an ineligible participant cannot perform the guarded transition, and approval evidence remains attached to the decision. Browser-local actor selections are workflow assertions rather than authenticated identities.

## Constitutional linkage

Each source record retains an exact excerpt, assignment reference, section, interpretation, assumption basis and reviewer. Executable rules are typed rather than inferred from unrestricted prose. Activation freezes source snapshots and a revision. Editing or removing a source invalidates the active rule set. The same policy governs allocation limits/screens, forecast checks and capital/governance approval thresholds.

This separates a family principle from an operational choice. For example, a principle to preserve liquidity does not itself establish a numerical cash floor; the floor must be recorded as an interpretation or modelling assumption and reviewed. The adopted numerical mandate and selected clauses are mapped in the [canonical trace](../03%20-%20Architecture/Trace.md).

## Estate and wealth structures

The estate workbench uses explicitly scoped English-law rule packs and dated eligibility inputs. Supported calculations include death-estate allowances and exemptions, qualifying residence taper, chronological cash gifts and taper relief, date-dependent business relief, and a separate simple relevant-property trust lifecycle. Unsupported case conditions withhold a total. Transfer timing and monthly funding scenarios expose tax/cost obligations and accessible resources.

Official sources are linked in the application beside the model. Legal eligibility is not inferred merely from an asset label. Holding-company ownership is represented through the governance graph and financial records; structural control, tax liability and actual access to cash are distinct considerations.

## Financial model and portfolio theory

Entity balance, income and cash records are translated and consolidated using matched elimination identifiers. Reconciliation checks preserve unmatched differences instead of hiding them. Opening, transaction and closing exchange rates separate translation effects from cash movements. Family wealth uses a non-overlapping owner perimeter and replaces the business carrying investment with a DCF equity valuation; consolidated book assets and fair-value family AUM are not interchangeable.

Business valuation includes discounted year-end free cash flows, a terminal value, an enterprise-to-equity bridge, a multiple comparison and discount/growth sensitivity. Cash forecasts are entity-specific: business profit does not become freely distributable family cash. Dated wealth snapshots distinguish external flows from changes in AUM.

Portfolio construction uses expected returns and a full validated correlation matrix. A continuous convex minimum-variance solver evaluates active constraints and traces the efficient frontier to maximise expected return within a volatility ceiling. It reports infeasibility, numerical residuals and the supported two-to-six-security scope. Current holdings, sector screens and illiquid restrictions remain explicit. Screened/unscreened comparison measures the opportunity-set effect under the entered assumptions, not a universal claim about ESG performance.

Consecutive monthly valuation records produce gross/net time-weighted returns, annualised risk and Sharpe, benchmark comparison, real-return adjustment and unitised drawdown. Brinson–Fachler allocation, selection and interaction effects are linked across periods with a reconciliation residual. Historical monthly VaR/CVaR retain their sample horizon and limitations.

Seeded correlated projections model spending, fees, gains-tax assumptions, liquidity restrictions and dated events. Internal generation transfers change economic interests; tax and transaction costs are separate wealth outflows. Depletion and reserve outcomes are conditional on assumptions, not predicted family outcomes.

## Office operating model

The office workspace combines owner/entity action queues, generated recurring occurrences, completion evidence and linked governance/service reviews. Editable service quotes support an SFO/MFO cost comparison with fixed costs, basis-point fees, setup and escalation assumptions. Grants and ventures move through diligence, governance approval and budget commitment; externally evidenced payments and refunds are recorded separately from approvals.

The board pack reads saved inputs from the same model owners and identifies missing or invalid records. Case packs support validation before restoration and preserve source-linked policy. Browser storage requires exported backups and does not provide collaborative production access control.

## AI use and critical evaluation

The [AI implementation record](../01%20-%20Notes/AI%20-%20Implementation%20Record.md) records actual task prompts and corrections. Early output was rejected because visual polish and isolated calculations did not satisfy the whole brief. Subsequent review corrected discrete optimisation, disconnected financial histories, FX assumptions, business-profit liquidity and double-counted wealth definitions. These examples demonstrate that AI-generated code was challenged rather than accepted as evidence of correctness.

The team should append genuine session excerpts/screenshots and its own contribution statement. This report does not invent team member actions, source selections, user research or historical prompts.

## Validation and interpretation

Current results and commands are recorded in the [integration verification](../03%20-%20Architecture/Integration%20-%20Family%20Office%20Workspaces.md). Focused model checks examine known allocation solutions, infeasibility, statement reconciliation, transfer-versus-cost treatment, return attribution, source invalidation, case restore and capital/report workflows. Rendered checks inspect meaningful user actions and mobile layout. These checks establish specific prototype behaviour; they do not certify investment assumptions, legal advice or production security.

Use the application's exported board pack after final population as the numerical results appendix. Explain what the selected family's results imply: concentration, required return feasibility, spending sustainability, funding gaps, governance readiness and the cost of chosen screens. Do not substitute the illustrative output for the team's final case analysis.
