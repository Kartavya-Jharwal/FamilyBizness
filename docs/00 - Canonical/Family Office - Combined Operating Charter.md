# Family Office — Combined Operating Charter

This is the selected fictional operating charter for the application, assembled from four supplied constitutions. It is one prepared family office, not a constitution-creation service or a consolidation of the four source businesses. English law and GBP remain the application baseline. The [product identity](Identity.md) owns the product facts. The existing module schemas and ownership register are populated with a GBP management-account scenario owned by `stewardship-scenario.ts`.

## 1. Family, owners and professional team

The prepared workspace is **Family stewardship office**, with fictional **Family Enterprise Holdings Ltd** and **Family Enterprise Operations Ltd**. Retain the existing illustrative ownership/economic inputs rather than adding the source families' share registers, revenue or asset values. The selected ownership register totals 100%; descendants and trust beneficiaries without registered shares receive no automatic shareholder votes. A family relationship does not grant executive or trustee authority.

The patriarch, family board, managing director, CEO, associate, legal counsel and family technician are visitor profiles for this same office. Their saved charter, planning inputs, operations, calendar and board evidence are shared. Persona selection is not production authentication or access control. Previously saved independent workspaces remain recoverable in a secondary entry section; they are not the primary offer.

## 2. Governance and decision rights

Use the selected five-member Council and five-member Board, including two independent directors, and a separate professional CEO. Retain monthly Council, quarterly Board, at least twice-yearly owners and annual Assembly meetings. These are selected cadences, not the sum of every donor's roster or meeting schedule.

Management handles routine operations within authority. The Board oversees strategy, budget, major investments and executive appointments. Owners exercise reserved ownership rights. The Council handles family policy, education, conduct and constitutional proposals. Conflicted participants declare the interest and recuse themselves.

The selected reserved-decision check requires 75% of eligible disinterested owner voting rights and a two-thirds Board recommendation. Quorum uses 50% of eligible voter headcount with the selected outside-largest-branch representation condition. Applicable company/instrument approvals remain separately acknowledged. Generic workflow reviewer counts do not replace these ownership tests.

## 3. Cash, distributions and reserves

Retain Golden Bird's explicit cash basis: fund twelve-month debt/statutory obligations, the approved family reserve and six months' fixed operating costs before discretionary distributions. Check solvency, covenants, the selected flagship compliance gate and profits lawfully available for dividends.

Allocate remaining distributable cash 50% to core reinvestment/reserves, 30% to permitted dividends and 20% to ring-fenced growth. The operating scenario funds a GBP 150,000 family-support reserve; the investment mandate separately protects GBP 800,000 of succession liquidity. Applying the budget updates the existing financial forecast and saved multi-generation spending scenario atomically.

Calcot's resilience-before-payout principle reinforces this choice. Sandoval's 25–40% and Vasnani's 30–40% **profit** payout bands are not additional cash percentages and are not converted into AUM withdrawal caps. Vasnani's 5% continuity contribution and 1% giving budget are not silently added to the selected allocation.

## 4. Ownership liquidity and transfers

Adapt Vasnani's staged first-offer review: seller's branch (21 days), other eligible shareholders (21), company buy-back consideration (60), then an approved employee trust (30) only where such a trust actually exists. These are workflow review stages, not automatic legal transfers. Do not imply that the composite has an EBT merely because the source does.

Review an affordable liquidity window of up to 5% of issued shares every three years, with the first planning review in 2027. Obtain independent valuation and preserve the no-minority-discount approach for compulsory-transfer review. Record eligible holders, funding, signed terms and competent approvals before implementation. The source's voluntary discount and interest/payment terms remain transaction-specific review matters, not a newly implemented pricing engine. Golden Bird's ten-year ordinary lock-up is not adopted alongside this liquidity route.

## 5. Sale-or-retain readiness

Use Calcot's 2028–2030 ownership-transition window as a **review scenario**. Prepare independent valuation, debt/tax analysis, employee transition, minority information/treatment and a written recommendation. A preparation approval is not shareholder approval to sell. Trust-held proceeds remain distinct from personal distributions.

If an acceptable sale is not pursued, maintain the business and evidence leadership succession, key-person retention and handover. The governance continuity register holds both readiness and fallback actions, including their prerequisites; neither is marked completed without evidence. No projected sale proceeds are booked or added to wealth.

## 6. Estate and continuity

Use the existing scoped, dated English-law estate/trust and liquidity engine. Keep legal owner, beneficial interest, registered authority and asset/funding timing separate. Review wills, LPAs, trust deeds, nominations, insurance and deputy mandates every three years and after material events. Scottish and Honduran legal procedures and Indian-law instruments are not imported into the composite. An England-and-Wales instrument review remains assigned through the existing source/operations workflows.

## 7. Family employment

Select three years' relevant external experience for management entry from Vasnani, rather than Sandoval's two-year minimum. Require an actual vacancy, relevant qualifications, standard recruitment and independent assessment. Use market pay, non-family performance review and avoidance of close-relative reporting. Neither beneficiary status nor family membership creates employment or promotion rights. The office records the assessment task and competent reviewers; it does not implement a separate HR system.

## 8. Next-generation development

Combine Sandoval's cross-branch/business mentoring and six-month rotations with Calcot's preparation for stewardship beyond a possible sale and Vasnani's staged ownership literacy. Record mentoring, financial/risk/valuation education, periodic development reviews and non-voting observation. Participation does not itself transfer shares or appointment rights.

## 9. Disputes and conduct

Select Vasnani's 14-day private-discussion target, followed by disinterested Council review and independent mediation. Record the matter, owner, conflict recusal, confidentiality and next action. Do not adopt a foreign arbitration seat or a blanket bar on urgent protection of rights. Formal resolution follows the applicable instruments. A scheduled readiness review is not a fabricated active family dispute.

## 10. Communication and calendar

Use Sandoval's papers seven days before meetings and minutes within ten days afterwards, plus Vasnani's material-owner-news target of 48 hours. Quarterly owner reporting and purpose-based family/owner/Board information remain distinct. A material-news deadline starts on the event; it is not represented as a recurring 48-hour task.

Meeting records, papers/minutes tasks, recurring reviews and owners use the existing operations register. Calendar agenda and week views show that same record set. The initial Board meeting is seven days after the case date so its paper and minute deadlines are consistent.

## 11. Community, values and investment

Select education/apprenticeships, community wellbeing, responsible business and sustainability review. Populate two illustrative draft grant requests with explicit pending diligence/approval. The £5,000 and £3,000 request amounts are modelling assumptions, not constitutional entitlements, approved commitments or tax-deductibility claims.

The Board adopts the application investment mandate: at least 10% investment cash, at most 65% public equities and 25% illiquid investments, a GBP 800,000 succession reserve, no controversial-weapons investments and two independent approvals for commitments above GBP 250,000. Target annual net return is 5% within 12% annual volatility. These are the composite Board's implementation decisions for this hypothetical office, traced to FO-IPS-MANDATE, rather than quotations attributed to the original four source families. The donated policy parser compiles the exposure sentences; the existing hard-rule engine enforces the resulting limits. Natural-language revisions are reviewed and activated through one transactional administration action.

## 12. Review and “please fix”

Review the charter every three years and after death/incapacity, ownership changes, material tax changes, a major dispute and sale/retention decisions. Changes retain source and revision evidence. Altering an adopted source invalidates the corresponding operating check until reviewed/adopted again.

The patriarch's literal **please fix** message remains a small corrective action to the associate or family technician for constitutional legality and instrument/source references, with recorded resolution evidence.

All surfaces use Portfolio components and canonical tokens.
