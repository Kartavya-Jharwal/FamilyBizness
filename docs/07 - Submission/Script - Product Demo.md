# Script - Product Demo

Proof-of-concept walkthrough, about three minutes spoken. Facts follow [Identity](../00%20-%20Canonical/Identity.md). Imports and external connections are UI previews only. Stochastic models are illustrative. On GitHub Pages, prefix every path with `/FamilyBizness`.

| # | On screen | Spoken | ~s |
|---|-----------|--------|----|
| 1 | Landing `/` — welcome dialog, click **Continue with the Smith demo**, then hero | "First visit asks once whose family office this is. We'll continue with the Smith demo. This is Smith Family Office. A legacy in motion: one private platform for a family business, its estate and the generations to come." | 15 |
| 2 | Landing — "More than what we own" story | "The Smith family is hypothetical. It began as a line of smiths who served a royal palace and held a fictional Royal Warrant. Craftsmanship and modest living still shape how they care for their wealth. Any visitor can read this story." | 25 |
| 3 | `/portfolio/?choose-profile=1#/office/case` | "Log in as a role: Patriarch, Family board, Managing Director or CEO, Associate or family technician, or Legal counsel." | 15 |
| 4 | `#/office/case` — Stewardship overview | "The Stewardship overview opens with the family's cover and dithered visuals. Brand first, then the work." | 15 |
| 5 | Profile picker again | "Every role enters the same prepared office. What changes is the navigation each person sees." | 10 |
| 6 | `#/office/policy`, then `#/office/constitution` | "The Investment Policy Statement is written in plain language and compiled into rules the platform can check. Those rules trace back to the Smith Family Constitution, the family's one charter." | 25 |
| 7 | `#/goals` | "Goals record what the family wants across generations, so each decision has a purpose." | 10 |
| 8 | `#/office/finance` → `#/office/statements` → `#/settings/connections` → `#/office/stochastic` → `#/office/simulation?tab=portfolio` | "Capital and liquidity, then financial statements. Under Integrations, quick-connect previews show where external APIs would plug in. The Stochastic Process Lab and Monte Carlo let us track illustrative models, not forecasts." | 35 |
| 9 | `#/settings/general` → `#/settings/appearance` → `#/settings/records` (Data) (show **Wipe and restart demo**, don't click) | "Settings hold the family name and appearance. One button wipes everything saved in this browser and restarts the demo." | 15 |
| 10 | Landing `#fork` — "Fork it. Run it on your own hardware." and **Copy prompt** | "Copy this prompt into your own Claude. It forks the repo, wires real APIs and sets it up on your own hardware, so a micro single-family office starts from this proof of concept." | 20 |
| 11 | Landing — "Built on open source. Kept in the open." band, then footer | "We built this from open-source projects, and it stays open source. Fellow small family offices can contribute to this forked, merged platform and help it grow." | 15 |
