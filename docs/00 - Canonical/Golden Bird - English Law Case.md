## Case convention

The application presents a populated England and Wales family holding group in pounds sterling. Source governance thresholds and the ordinary ownership split are retained. Fictional appointments, beneficiaries, operating budgets and estate values make the case usable without implying that the teaching document describes real transactions or executed instruments. These assumptions live in the adaptation fixture rather than appearing as repetitive warnings in the interface.

All original INR amounts use the single dated official administrative reference recorded in `goldenBirdFx`. The conversion is INR divided by the stated INR-per-pound rate. Calculations retain precision; displayed currency rounds to pence, so displayed subscription rows may have a penny rounding residual. The rate is a reproducible fictional-case convention, not a live dealing quote. The [November 2026 FCDO consular exchange table](https://www.gov.uk/csv-preview/6ae4b56f3d3bf10d23546232/consular-exchange-rates-November-2026.csv) is the primary rate source.

For the fictional English company, ordinary shares use a chosen 1p nominal denomination; total converted funding is retained, with the balance treated as share premium. The fixture also preserves converted original subscription, nominal and premium unit values for audit. This is an explicit capitalization choice rather than changing shareholder economic percentages or mistaking nominal capital for the whole investment.

## Legal port

The private company and its assets are distinct from shareholders. Company articles and a shareholders' agreement implement the selected transfer, pre-emption and decision rules. The source's disinterested-owner voting gate remains a contractual case requirement; it is not relabelled as the statutory special-resolution denominator. The [Companies Act 2006 section 283](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46/section/283) sets the statutory resolution framework separately.

The cash waterfall adds the English company-law distributable-profit requirement to the source liquidity tests. Cash in a bank account or consolidated accounting value does not establish profits legally available for a dividend. The statutory foundation is [Companies Act 2006 section 830](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2006/46/section/830); the selected family allocation applies only after those legal and case cash gates.

HUF and Indian coparcenary concepts are not carried into the application case. Personal property, company shares and assets settled into a trust have separate ownership and authority records. A will names executors for estate administration after death; a registered property and financial affairs lasting power of attorney supplies a separate lifetime personal-finance mandate. It does not confer a company directorship or permit changing the donor's will. [GOV.UK attorney guidance](https://www.gov.uk/manage-lasting-power-attorney/property-financial-affairs) supports that separation.

The chosen family settlement is an England and Wales discretionary trust with independently held trustee authority. Potential beneficiaries do not automatically own or vote its assets. The trust deed determines trustee decisions and beneficiary interests, consistent with [GOV.UK trusts guidance](https://www.gov.uk/trusts-taxes) and the [types of trust](https://www.gov.uk/trusts-taxes/types-of-trust) guidance. The fixture's separate cash-settlement scenario does not also count as personal estate cash or corporate subscription capital.
